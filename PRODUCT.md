# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no framework). Confirmed by the owner at init.

## Users

The owner is the primary user: a personal project, written and read by the person who builds it.

Open: whether the content will later be published for other readers, and who they would be. Do not design for an imagined outside audience until this is decided.

## Product Purpose

A documentation and content site: a place to write and read articles, notes or guides.

Open: the subject matter and what success looks like for the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static content pages served by Vite (`npm run dev`, `npm run build`).
- Entry point is `index.html`, with styles in `src/style.css` and script in `src/main.js`.
- Open: content format (hand-written HTML vs Markdown), navigation structure, search, and deploy target.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The project is a fresh scaffold with placeholder copy. Do not invent articles, authors, dates or other content.

## Product Principles

1. Reading comes first: content stays legible and easy to scan before anything else.
2. Stay lightweight: prefer plain HTML and CSS over adding dependencies.
3. Record decisions as they are made; leave open questions open rather than guessing.

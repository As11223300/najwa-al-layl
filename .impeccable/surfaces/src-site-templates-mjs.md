## Scope

The whole site «نجوى الليل»: home (current issue cover), poem page, article page, collections, writing index with search, about, and the novel section (book page with cover, synopsis, chapter index and bookmarks; chapter reader). Mode: Read on every surface.

## Audience and job

Public Arabic readers arriving by link or search, often on a phone at night. Read one piece, then wander to another; follow the novel chapter by chapter and come back to where they stopped. Hundreds of pieces; the author adds real writing later, so sample pieces are labeled placeholders.

## Constraints

RTL, Arabic-Indic digits, free verse with exact line breaks, Vite + plain JS, static output for GitHub Pages, fonts served from the project. Pinned by the author: name «نجوى الليل», a darker midnight ink blue, warm vintage paper textures, elegant Arabic calligraphy, subtle celestial details, an intimate and timeless literary-journal feel, as if discovered on a quiet winter night; curated seasonal and thematic collections as the issues; a novel published chapter by chapter with bookmarks and night reading.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one writer's night journal, set like a modernist Arabic poetry review: every curated collection is an issue, the home page is the current issue's cover with its contents as the picture, and the novel is a bound book inside the journal. It refuses the blog feed of cards and the orientalist arabesque frame.

OWN-WORLD: Warm vintage paper ground with real paper grain, one midnight ink that owns whole fields (issue covers, book cover, colophon) plus blue-black text. Titles in Ruq'a calligraphy, reading in a Bulaq-style naskh, small labels in a plain naskh. Each cover carries its own faint constellation (one star per piece) and a single four-point star ends a poem; a crescent marks the colophon. No arabesque, no gradients, no glow.

STORY: The reader opens a quiet winter-night journal by one voice, meets the current issue's contents, reads a poem or a chapter, and is carried onward by the issue's order or the novel's next chapter, finding their place kept.
